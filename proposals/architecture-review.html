Verdict

Three subsystems: locks in the bucket (ADR-0112: locks/<kind>-<instant>, write first look second, pid liveness, --wait, unlock, the cleanup top-up), the restore rework (ADR-0109 full path under --output, ADR-0110 paths, a list or --all, ADR-0111 extract), and the reader's validity rules from three clean-room sets (#425 torn UTF-8, #428 one tree as files, #439/#452–#457 the spec's gaps). Beside them ADR-0113's basic-form names, #459's digest-before-completion, #460's uninspectable entries, #463's early temp.

The new modules are deep. locks.mjs holds the whole rule behind two guards that take only a closure, and nothing outside it re-derives liveness; extract composes restoreTargets rather than carrying a loop. The friction is at the joins and in what the tests don't say: three of the six lock sites and the upload --file guard that #459 changed are asserted nowhere, though the fakes already exist; the cleanup top-up is stated in four modules and its interlocks run twice; a restore fault kind is five hand-enumerations. The carried list holds — the one-reader divergences were run this pass, not traced — and two entries got wider (the walk's classifier, surplus positionals), one dead.
